CLUTCH · Built Different

BUILT DIFFERENT ISN'T A SLOGAN.

I'm Sebastian, 13. Upper East Side.

Basketball is the whole thing for me. Point guard. Pick and roll, reading the defense before it happens, running the team. I study Brunson for that, the way he controls a game without forcing anything. Same way I move on the court is the same way I built this.

Then there's the rest: school, student council, the gym, hoops, CLUTCH. All in the same week. Nobody told me to start a clothing company, I just did it. Most of it happens late, after everyone else is asleep. Some nights I'm still on the site at 3am.

I wanted shorts that actually looked like where I'm from, so I mapped my whole neighborhood and put it on mesh.

Making them was the hard part. The first print came back with the map stretched out of shape, so I rebuilt the file from scratch, clean lines, straight grid, logo exactly where it belongs, until the print finally sat right.

Then the easy way to sell them fell apart. No Stripe, no normal checkout. Most people would've quit right there. I rebuilt the whole drop around DMs instead. 30 pairs. One run. No restocks.

That's why the script inside the pocket says "clutch built different (on the inside)." The outside is the map. The inside is everything it took to get here.

— Sebastian Gallegos

Drop 002 / FORM 001

Drop 002 is FORM 001, and it's a different world from Drop 001. No map this time. This one is the 6 train.

Forest green all around. The cream OG logo sits low on one leg. One traditional Anthora cup sits low on the opposite leg — blue and white, Greek key border, "WE ARE HAPPY TO SERVE YOU." Brick waistband. Greek-key hem. The back is plain forest-green mesh.

Inside, not outside

Nothing about the drop is printed on the outside of the shorts. All of it — CLUTCH, DROP 002, FORM 001, LEXINGTON AVE LOCAL, and "built different (on the inside)" — lives on the label inside.

The drop

40 pairs, $40 a pair. Below-knee, oversized fit. No date yet — the drop opens after Drop 001 sells out. Check the Drop 002 page for the latest.

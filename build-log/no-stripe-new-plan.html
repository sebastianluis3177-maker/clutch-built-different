No Stripe, New Plan

Then the easy way to sell them fell apart. No Stripe, no normal checkout. Most people would've quit there.

I rebuilt the whole drop around DMs and Greenlight instead. You DM @clutchny1 on Instagram to buy, and pay through the Greenlight link. $25 for the shorts, $10 shipping, $35 total. All sales final.

Why it works

The drop is small — 30 pairs, one run, no restocks. Every order is personal. You talk to the person who made the shorts, not a checkout page. That's slower, but it's real.

The Greenlight link is on the site and in the checkout. Pickup in New York City is free. If you pay less than the total, the shorts don't ship — that's the rule, and it's on the checkout page.

Build Log · November 2026

The Idea

I'm Sebastian. Upper East Side. I grew up on these blocks — the 6 train, the corner store, the courts after school.

One day I wanted shorts that actually looked like where I'm from. Not a logo slapped on a blank pair — the actual neighborhood. So I mapped the whole Upper East Side and put it on mesh.

Why a map

The map is the real street grid. If you know the Upper East Side, you can read it — the avenues, the cross streets, the park on the west side. That's the whole point. The shorts are the neighborhood, wearable.

Why mesh basketball shorts

Mesh shorts are the uniform. Everybody hoops in them, everybody wears them out. They had to be real basketball shorts — not fashion shorts pretending. Oversized fit, drawstring, pockets. The pocket bags carry the script on the inside: "clutch built different (on the inside)."

The name is CLUTCH. Built Different. The outside is the map. The inside is everything it took to get here.

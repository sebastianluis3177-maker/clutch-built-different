Build Log · November 2026

The Packaging

Every order goes out in a black 10x13 poly mailer. No big box, no waste — the shorts fold in, the mailer seals, and it ships USPS Ground Advantage.

Inside every order is a kraft thank-you card. Small thing, but it matters — somebody bought the first run of a brand a 7th grader built, and that deserves a real thanks, not a printed receipt.

Labels, weighed right

Labels get printed at home on regular paper, cut out, and taped on flat so the barcode scans. The packed mailer gets weighed at the post office once, so every label after that has the right weight on it.
